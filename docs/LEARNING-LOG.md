# Learning Log

**This log is yours.** Fill it in yourself when you notice something in the markets, the
news or the economy. Real, dated observations in your own words are exactly what makes a
personal statement or interview answer stand out ("In March I noticed gilt yields rose
after..., which I think was because...").

Tips:
- Write the entry the same day, while it's fresh. Short is fine.
- "Why I think it happened" is a hypothesis - it's fine to be wrong. Revisit it later.
- "Link to syllabus" = the A-level / degree topic it connects to (e.g. *AQA Econ 4.2.4
  Monetary policy*, *aggregate demand*, *PED*, *IAS 16 depreciation*).
- You can also use the **My take** notes on each instrument in the app, then copy the best
  ones here.
